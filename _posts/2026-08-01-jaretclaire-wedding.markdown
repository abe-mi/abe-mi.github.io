## My Best Man Speech
![Best Man Speech]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/bestman_speech.jpg)


Good evening, everyone. For those of you who don't know me, I'm Logan, Jaret's younger brother.
When I started thinking about what I wanted to say today, I found myself going back to a lot of childhood memories.
Some of my favorite memories growing up alongside my brother happened at my Aunt Rhonda's house with our cousins, Megan and Abbey. Growing up, it felt like we spent every day there.
Megan told me something recently that I'd never really thought about before. She said, "It always felt like a competition between the four of us: Abbey and me versus Jaret and Megan."
Whether we were playing Sly Cooper, Guitar Hero, or Pac-Man, racing the golf cart through the trails, sledding down the hill in the backyard, or getting excited every time the Schwans Man pulled into the driveway with ice cream, somehow everything always turned into a competition.


As we got older though, it felt like we were always on separate teams.
Looking back,… I'm pretty sure me deciding to wear green instead of blue every football season didn't help that much either.
It wasn't until a few years ago that I really felt like Jaret and I started growing closer.
For me, it started one weekend while I was home from college. My dad and I were sitting on the back deck, looking at the water, having a drink, and just talking.
There was a point where I remember telling him that, somewhere along the way, it felt like Jaret and I had just gotten used to being on separate teams. I think we had always cared about each other, but I wondered if we would always do things separately.
My dad told me something that day that I'm not even sure he remembers, but it really changed my perspective of my brother.
He told me that he would overhear Jaret talking with customers at work, and whenever someone asked about his family, he'd proudly tell them about his little brother who was going to college at Michigan State.
For those of you who don't know Jaret, he isn't the type to tell you how he's feeling, so hearing that he was proud of me for going to college caught me completely off guard. Not because I doubted my brother, but because it had never been anything but rivalry between us.
It made me realize that I'd spent a lot of time thinking about the moments Jaret and I didn't connect, and not nearly enough time appreciating all the quiet ways he'd always been my older brother.


As we've gotten older, one of my favorite things we've started doing together is golfing. While I've accepted that I'm probably never going to beat Jaret, winning has stopped mattering so much to me. I just enjoy getting to spend the time with him and hear about him and Claire.

As Jaret and I have spent more time around each other and grown closer over the last few years, I've been able to see and hear so much about the life the two of you have built together.

Claire,… one thing I've noticed over the last few years is that I've never seen Jaret happier than he is when he is around you. I get to hear about your adventures together and see how you've turned my brother into the proudest of cat dads. I see how happy you make him. You've helped bring out a soft side of him that I don't remember seeing much growing up, and it's been really special to watch.
Jaret,… I don't think I've ever told you this directly, but I hope you know how much I respect the man you've become. You've always had a huge heart, you've always been willing to put other people before yourself, and the older I get, the more I appreciate having you as my older brother.
I wish you both nothing but happiness in this next chapter together.
If everyone would please raise a glass.
To Jaret and Claire—cheers!

E
